## Vision

To provide a **modular Vanilla+ expansion of Stardew Valley** that adds more content, variety, quality-of-life improvements, and customization while preserving the charm and identity of the original game.

**Potluck brings the dishes. You decide what goes on your plate.**

---

## What is Potluck?

Potluck is a curated **Wabbajack Mod List** for Stardew Valley.

It combines a large collection of compatible Stardew Valley and SMAPI mods into an organized Mod Organizer 2 installation with a tested default configuration.

The goal is simple:

> **Spend less time managing mods and more time in the Valley.**

---

## Compatibility

- **Game:** Stardew Valley
- **Platform:** PC, Windows 10/11, Steam
- **Distribution:** Wabbajack
- **Mod Manager:** Mod Organizer 2
- **Mod Framework:** SMAPI

Your installed game version must match the version currently supported by Potluck.

---

## Distinctive Features

### 🍲 Modular

Many included mods can be enabled or disabled through Mod Organizer 2, allowing you to customize Potluck without maintaining an entirely separate installation.

### 🧰 Organized Through Mod Organizer 2

Mods are organized through MO2 to make customization, troubleshooting, and maintenance easier.

### 📝 Helpful MO2 Notes

Included mods have concise MO2 Notes explaining what they do and, where applicable, requirements, incompatibilities, and other useful information.

### 🧪 Tested Default Configuration

Potluck includes a default profile representing the configuration primarily developed, tested, and supported by the project.

---

## Mod Organizer 2

Potluck uses **Mod Organizer 2 (MO2)** to manage its mods and configuration.

You do not need to be an MO2 expert to play Potluck. The supplied default profile is intended to provide a ready-to-play configuration.

---

## Included Mods

Potluck includes mods covering areas such as:

- Gameplay
- Farming
- Fishing
- Mining
- NPCs and Relationships
- New Content
- Visuals
- User Interface
- Quality of Life
- Immersion
- Bug Fixes

Not every included mod is necessarily enabled by default.

---

## Requirements

At minimum, you will need:

- A legitimate Steam installation of **Stardew Valley**
- The currently supported game version
- **Wabbajack**
- Sufficient storage space for the game, downloads, and installed mod list

A **Nexus Mods Premium** account is strongly recommended for automated Wabbajack downloads.
