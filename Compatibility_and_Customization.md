Potluck is designed to be modular, but the supplied **Default profile** is the configuration primarily tested and supported by the project.

## Before Customizing

Before changing Potluck:

1. Launch and test the supplied **Default profile** first.
2. Confirm Potluck works correctly.
3. Create your own MO2 profile.
4. Make your changes there.

**Do not customize the Default profile.** Keeping it unchanged gives you a known-good configuration for testing and troubleshooting.

---

## MO2 Notes

Many mods include useful information in the **Notes** column in Mod Organizer 2.

Notes may identify:

- Requirements
- Incompatibilities
- Recommended options
- Important configuration information

**Read the Notes before enabling or disabling a mod.**

---

## Enabling and Disabling Mods

Many included mods are optional and can be toggled through MO2.

However, optional does not mean every possible combination is compatible.

Before enabling a mod, check its Notes and any listed requirements or incompatibilities.

---

## Compatibility Checker

Potluck includes the **Potluck Compatibility Checker** for Mod Organizer 2.

Use it after changing your profile to check for known:

- Missing requirements
- Incompatible combinations
- Configuration problems

The checker can identify **known rules**, but it cannot guarantee that every possible combination of mods is compatible.

---

## Adding Your Own Mods

You are welcome to add mods that are not included with Potluck.

Install them through **Mod Organizer 2** rather than manually placing them into the Stardew Valley game directory or AppData Mods folders.

Off-list mods are not tested as part of Potluck and may introduce conflicts or other problems.

---

## Save Compatibility

Be careful when adding or removing mods from an existing save.

Mods that add locations, NPCs, items, events, quests, relationships, or other persistent content may not be safe to remove mid-playthrough.

Check the original mod author's documentation before adding or removing a mod from an existing save.

When in doubt, make a backup first.

---

## Getting Support

If you encounter a problem after customizing Potluck, first reproduce the issue using the unchanged **Default profile**.

If the problem does not occur on Default, the issue is likely related to your customized configuration.

When asking for help, clearly identify any mods you have added, removed, updated, enabled, or disabled.
